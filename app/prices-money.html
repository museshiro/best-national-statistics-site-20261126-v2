Policy rate vs inflation % per month, selected year — BI-Rate vs CPI inflation (y-on-y)

Bank Indonesia raised the rate to 6.25% in April 2024 as the rupiah weakened, then cut back to 6.00% in September as inflation fell below 2%.

Rupiah per US dollar annual average, 2020–2024

The rupiah averaged Rp15,855 per US$ in 2024, its weakest annual average of the five years.

Foreign-exchange reserves US$ billion, end of year, 2020–2024

Reserves rose from US$135.9 bn in 2020 to a record US$155.7 bn at end-2024 — about 6 months of imports.

Monthly inflation CPI, % y-on-y, selected year

Inflation fell steadily through 2024, from 2.57% in January to 1.57% in December.
