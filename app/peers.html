Reading the comparison

Size and prosperity tell different stories: Indonesia leads ASEAN on total output and population, but its income per person trails Malaysia (US$11,874) and Thailand (US$7,387) — the gap that development policy is trying to close. Growth, though, is on Indonesia's side: 5.03% in 2024, second only to Vietnam and the Philippines in this group.
