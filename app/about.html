About & Data Dictionary

Indonesia in Numbers is an independent editorial almanac of Indonesian statistics — a static snapshot that runs entirely offline, with no network requests, no tracking and no cookies. It is not affiliated with BPS or any government body.

Data vintage & conventions

  • Reference year is 2024 (BPS releases of February 2025). Exceptions are labelled: UMP uses 2025 (set in December 2024), literacy uses 2023.
  • “≈” means approximately — a rounded or estimated figure. “—” means the figure is not shown because it is unverified.
  • Units: T = trillion, B = billion, M = million. Rupiah figures use Indonesian formatting (Rp22,139T = 22,139 trillion rupiah).
  • Decimal separator: this site uses a point (5.03%), following international statistical style.
  • World Bank vs BPS population: the peer page uses World Bank population (283.5M) for all six countries; BPS reports 281.6M for Indonesia.

Definitions

IndicatorDefinition
Real GDP growthYear-on-year change in GDP at constant prices (c-to-c for the annual figure).
GDP per capitaNominal GDP divided by mid-year population.

HDIHuman Development Index (0–100), combining health, education and income.
Gini ratioInequality measure from 0 (perfect equality) to 1; March 2024 reading.
Open unemployment rate (TPT)Unemployed as a share of the labour force, August 2024.
BI-RateBank Indonesia's policy interest rate.
UMPProvincial minimum wage (Upah Minimum Provinsi), set annually.
GRDP (PDRB)Gross Regional Domestic Product — GDP measured at the provincial level.
