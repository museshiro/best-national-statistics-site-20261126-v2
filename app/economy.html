GDP & Growth

Indonesia's economy reached Rp22,139 trillion in 2024 — about US$1.40 trillion, or US$4.67 trillion at purchasing-power parity (IMF estimate). Real growth was 5.03%, the third straight year above 5%.

Why it matters: Indonesia is the world's 16th-largest economy in nominal terms and 8th by purchasing-power parity — and the only G20 economy in Southeast Asia.

Nominal GDP Rp trillion, 2015 to selected year

Nominal GDP nearly doubled in a decade — Rp11,526 T in 2015 to Rp22,139 T in 2024 — despite the 2020 pandemic dip.

Real GDP growth % per year, 2015–2024

−2.07% in 2020 (COVID-19) and 5.03% in 2024 bracket the decade. The 2015–2019 average was about 5.0% a year.

GDP by sector share of GDP, 2024 — all 13 sectors

Manufacturing (19.0%), trade (12.8%) and agriculture (12.6%) are the three largest sectors. “Other services” (~9.3%) closes the total to 100%.
