Foreign Trade

Indonesia exported US$264.7 billion of goods in 2024 and imported US$233.7 billion — a surplus of US$31.0 billion, the fifth straight year in surplus. Coal, palm oil and steel lead the export list; China takes nearly a quarter of all exports.
