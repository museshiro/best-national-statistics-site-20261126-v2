About & Data Dictionary

Indonesia in Numbers is a static snapshot of Indonesia's official statistics, built to be explored: eight pages, 26 charts, and every figure traceable to its source. It makes no network requests — every chart is inline SVG and every number lives in the site's own files.

Sources

  • BPS — Statistics Indonesia (bps.go.id): GDP, population, inflation, unemployment, poverty, Gini, HDI, GRDP. Key releases: BRS No. 17/02/Th. XXVIII (GDP, 5 Feb 2025) and BRS No. 07/01/Th. XXVIII (poverty, 15 Jan 2025).
  • Bank Indonesia (bi.go.id): BI-Rate, foreign-exchange reserves, exchange rate.
  • Ministry of Manpower: provincial minimum wage (UMP) 2025, under Permenaker No. 16/2024 (national average +6.5%).
  • World Bank: goods trade 2020–2024 (exports, imports, partners, commodities).
  • IMF / CIA World Factbook: GDP at purchasing-power parity (IMF estimate, US$4.67 T) and the annual-average exchange rate.

Data vintage & conventions

  • Vintage 2024 for all headline figures, from BPS releases of February 2025. The single exception is the minimum wage, which is 2025 (set in December 2024) and is labelled as such everywhere it appears.
  • Units: Rp T = trillion rupiah; Rp mn = million rupiah; bn = billion US dollars; mn = million people. “c-to-c” means cumulative-to-cumulative (full-year growth).
  • “≈” marks a rounded or derived figure (for example GRDP per capita derived from rounded GRDP and population). Exact BPS figures carry no mark.
  • Poverty: September 2024 is 8.57% (March 2024 was 9.03%). The 8.57% figure is the lowest since records began in 1960.
  • Unverified, not shown: the 2025 minimum wage for Highland Papua (Papua Pegunungan) could not be verified and is omitted rather than guessed.
  • Static snapshot: this site is plain files under app/ — no build step, no fonts, no analytics, no images from the network, no requests of any kind.

Definitions

Nominal GDP
Gross domestic product at current prices, in rupiah. 2024: Rp22,139 trillion (≈ US$1.40 trillion).
Real GDP growth (c-to-c)
Year-on-year change in GDP at constant prices for the full year. 2024: 5.03%.
GDP per capita
Nominal GDP divided by mid-year population. 2024: Rp78.6 million (≈ US$4,960).
Inflation (CPI, y-on-y)
Change in the consumer price index versus the same month a year earlier. December 2024: 1.57%.
Open unemployment rate (TPT)
Unemployed as a share of the labour force. August 2024: 4.91%.
Trade balance
Exports of goods minus imports of goods. 2024: US$264.7 bn − US$233.7 bn = US$31.0 bn surplus.
GRDP (PDRB)
Gross Regional Domestic Product — GDP measured for a province. Jakarta 2024: ≈ Rp3,680 trillion.
UMP
Upah Minimum Provinsi — the provincial minimum wage, set annually. 2025: Rp2,169,349 (Central Java, lowest verified) to Rp5,396,761 (Jakarta).
Human Development Index (IPM)
BPS composite of health, education and living standards (0–100). 2024: 75.02.
Gini ratio
Measure of income inequality (0 = perfect equality). March 2024: 0.379.
Poverty line
Minimum monthly expenditure per capita to meet basic needs. September 2024: Rp595,242.
BI-Rate
Bank Indonesia's policy interest rate. December 2024: 6.00%.
FX reserves
Bank Indonesia's foreign-exchange reserves. December 2024: US$155.7 billion.
