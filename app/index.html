Indonesia at a glance. In 2024: growth 5.03%, population 281.6 million, inflation 1.57%, unemployment 4.91%. The largest economy in ASEAN, the 4th most populous nation on earth, and a G20 member whose economy grew every year of the last decade except the pandemic year 2020 (−2.07%). Poverty fell to 8.57% in September 2024 — the lowest since records began in 1960.
