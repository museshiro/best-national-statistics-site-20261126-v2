Regional Economy & Wages

Jakarta's economy (Rp3,680 T) is larger than the next two provinces combined — but per person, resource-rich East Kalimantan is second only to the capital. The 2025 minimum wage ranges from Rp2,169,349 in Central Java to Rp5,396,761 in Jakarta.
