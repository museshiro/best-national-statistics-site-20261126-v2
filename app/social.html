Welfare & Inequality

The headline of 2024: poverty fell to 8.57% — 24.06 million people, the lowest rate since records began in 1960. Human development reached 75.02, inequality eased to a Gini of 0.379, and open unemployment fell to 4.91%.

Behind the numbers: literacy (age 15+) is 96.70% (2023 Susenas), life expectancy is 74.15 years, and the poverty line is Rp595,242 per capita per month. Unemployment (open rate, August 2024) is 4.91% — down from 7.07% at the pandemic peak in 2020.

Human Development Index 2015–2024

HDI rose from 69.55 in 2015 to 75.02 in 2024 — Indonesia is now in the UN's “high human development” group.

Gini ratio 2015–2024 (lower = more equal)

Inequality fell from 0.408 in 2015 to 0.379 in March 2024 and has been flat since 2022.

Poverty rate % of population, September each year, 2015–2024

Poverty fell from about 11.1% in 2015 to 8.57% in September 2024 — 24.06 million people. The poverty line is Rp595,242 per capita per month.

Highest HDI by province 2024 (≈)

Jakarta (84.15) leads; Yogyakarta (81.62) and East Kalimantan (80.34) are the only others above 80.

Lowest HDI by province 2024 (≈)

The gap is stark: Central Papua (61.94) is more than 22 points below Jakarta — the development frontier is the eastern highlands.
